# Little Steps Parent Hub

A simple, mobile-friendly web app for parents of 4 to 6-year-olds getting ready for Grade 1.
Parents can see session dates, book a free taster or the full series, pay by VietQR bank transfer,
and open take-home activities in English and Vietnamese.

## How to edit

Everything you are likely to change (prices, dates, venue, Zalo number, bank details) is in the
`CONFIG` block near the top of the script in `index.html`. On GitHub, open `index.html`, click the
pencil icon, change the values, and click **Commit changes**. The live site updates within a minute or two.

Set `preview: false` once the dates, venue and payment details are real.
